RELATIONSHIPS:
- Membre and Entraineur inherit from Personne (inheritance)
- Equipe has a coach (Entraineur) - association
- Equipe has multiple membres (Membre) - aggregation
- Seance is associated with an Equipe - association
- Seance has a coach - association
- Paiement is associated with a Membre - association
- ClubSportif manages all entities - composition
